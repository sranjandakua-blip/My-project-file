## Lesson 01: The Grid · `ep01-grid/renders/ep01-grid.mp4`
**Caption:** Lesson 01 📐 Turn on grid lines (Camera settings → Grid lines) and put your subject where the lines cross, not dead center. Instantly more balanced photos. Shot on S26 Ultra.

## Lesson 02: Tap to Focus · `ep02-tap-to-focus/renders/ep02-tap-to-focus.mp4`
**Caption:** Lesson 02 🎯 Your phone guesses where to focus, and sometimes it guesses wrong. Tap your subject to focus, then drag the little ☀️ slider: down to save bright skies, up to lift the shadows.
**Hashtags:** #phonephotography #taptofocus #exposure #mobilephotography #photographytips #galaxys26ultra #photographyforbeginners #learnphotography

## Lesson 03: Clean Your Lens · `ep03-clean-lens/renders/ep03-clean-lens.mp4`
**Caption:** Lesson 03 🧼 The easiest upgrade there is. Fingerprints on the lens make lights glow and details go soft. Give it a quick wipe with a microfiber cloth (not your shirt) before every shot.
**Hashtags:** #phonephotography #mobilephotography #photographytips #cleanyourlens #galaxys26ultra #photographyhacks #photographyforbeginners #learnphotography

## Lesson 04: The Zoom Lenses · `ep04-zoom-lenses/renders/ep04-zoom-lenses.mp4`
**Caption:** Lesson 04 🔭 Four cameras, four jobs. .6x for big landscapes, 1x for everyday shots, 3x for flattering portraits, 5x for far-away details. Tap the zoom chips instead of pinching.
**Hashtags:** #phonephotography #zoomlens #telephoto #ultrawide #galaxys26ultra #mobilephotography #photographytips #learnphotography

## Lesson 05: Leading Lines · `ep05-leading-lines/renders/ep05-leading-lines.mp4`
**Caption:** Lesson 05 🛤️ Roads, rails, fences and rivers all pull the eye. Frame them so they run toward your subject, not off the edge of the photo.
