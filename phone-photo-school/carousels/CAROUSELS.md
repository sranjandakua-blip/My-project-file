# Phone Photo School: carousels + posting plan

Slides: `carousels/out/LNN/slide-*.jpg` (1080×1350, 4:5). Rebuild with `python3 carousels/make_carousels.py [lesson numbers]`.
Text and photos for every slide live in the `LESSONS` table at the top of `make_carousels.py`.

## Posting rhythm (alternate days, rest on Sunday)
Reel of a lesson one day → carousel of the same lesson the next day (it recaps the Reel and its last slide sends people back to it).

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reel 01 | Carousel 01 | Reel 02 | Carousel 02 | Reel 03 | Carousel 03 |
| 2 | Reel 04 | Carousel 04 | Reel 05 | Carousel 05 | Reel 06 | Carousel 06 |
| 3 | Reel 07 | Carousel 07 | Reel 08 | Carousel 08 | Reel 09 | Carousel 09 |
| 4 | Reel 10 | Carousel 10 | (Lesson 11 starts) | | | |

Tip: carousels get a second chance in the feed. If someone skips slide 1, Instagram often shows them slide 2 later, so every slide 2 works as a hook on its own.
